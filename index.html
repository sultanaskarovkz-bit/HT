Premier yacht berthing at HK Marina — your secure haven in Sai Kung.

Experience superior yacht berthing in Sai Kung at Hong Kong Marina. Our protected harbour, combined with deep-water frontages, offers secure and flexible boat mooring solutions tailored for long-term members, seasonal owners, and stopover guests alike. Discover the ultimate peace of mind for your vessel.

01 16 — 40 ft Inner Quay Berths

Our sheltered finger-pier berths are perfectly suited for day cruisers, sport fishers, and weekend craft. Each berth is equipped with power and potable water, complemented by our vigilant night-watch presence, ensuring the safety and convenience of your vessel.

02 40 — 80 ft Premium Frontage Berths

Designed for owners who frequently entertain aboard, these premium berths offer wider beams and deeper drafts. Benefit from direct shore access and dedicated provisioning services, enhancing your luxury yachting experience in Hong Kong.

03 80 — 100+ ft Superyacht Berths

Discover our prime superyacht berths with direct sea access and high-capacity power. These exclusive positions come with comprehensive concierge support, catering to the unique needs of international vessels and their discerning crews. We provide unparalleled superyacht services in Hong Kong.

04Home · About

A legacy of excellence, on the Sai Kung shore.

Established in 1963, Hong Kong Marina has for decades been more than just a luxury marina in Sai Kung; it’s a vibrant community built around enduring relationships. We cherish the owners who have chosen to berth their vessels with us for generations, the families who consider our dining room an extension of their home, and the dedicated crews we know by name. Our story is woven into the fabric of the Sai Kung yachting community, a testament to quiet dedication and unparalleled service.

Our story is unhurried, in the same way that good harbours are unhurried.

Founded in 1963 as a small, sheltered berth on the Sai Kung peninsula, Hong Kong Marina has grown over six decades into one of Asia’s most prestigious settings for private yachting. Through every chapter, the brief has remained the same: privacy, attentiveness, and a quiet sense of place.

The peninsula itself does much of the work. Granite islands break the open sea. The Three Sisters keep the cove still. The late-afternoon light is unlike anywhere else along the coast.

“A second home in Hong Kong, kept always ready for your return.”

Today the marina holds 190 berths, an exclusive clubhouse, a waterfront restaurant, and the small daily rituals of a community formed slowly and intentionally. We invite owners and their guests to step into that rhythm.

A private tour begins at the gate, moves through the basin, ends at the dining room — and, more often than not, lasts longer than planned.

Mission & Vision

Custodians of Port Shelter.

Our Mission

Hong Kong Marina is to provide discerning yacht and superyacht owners with secure, full-service berthing in the heart of Sai Kung’s Port Shelter, combining world-class marina facilities with the warmth and privacy of an exclusive members’ club. We are committed to delivering meticulous, discreet service and a tranquil country-retreat atmosphere, so that every moment spent at berth in Hong Kong is safe, effortless and truly enjoyable.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be Hong Kong’s most sought-after private superyacht marina and yacht club, renowned for exceptional berthing facilities, trusted service and an intimate waterfront community set amidst the natural beauty of Sai Kung. As Hong Kong grows as a regional yachting and superyacht hub, we aim to remain the preferred home port in Port Shelter for owners seeking a secure, environmentally respectful and effortlessly connected base in Asia.
